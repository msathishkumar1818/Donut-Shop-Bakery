Walk-Ins Welcome

Do I need to pre-order for daily counter visits?

No reservation or pre-order is needed for individual pastries, coffee, or boxes up to 6 donuts. We continuously fry and kettle-glaze fresh batches between 6:30 AM and 2:00 PM. For boxes of 12 or more, we recommend calling 2 hours ahead.

Allergen Safety

How are nut and gluten allergies managed?

Our brioche dough contains organic wheat flour, free-range eggs, and pasture butter. While we bake certified gluten-free options during a sanitized morning batch, our open kitchen actively handles pistachio, hazelnut, and tree nuts daily.

Tasting Flights

Can we schedule an in-person event tasting?

For weddings, galas, and corporate events with 50+ guests, private 45-minute tasting flights at our Hawthorne Garden kitchen every Wednesday and Saturday. You will sample custom glaze profiles and review presentation .

Courier Delivery

What are your delivery zones courier standards?

We provide climate-stabilized courier dispatch within a 15-mile radius of our Pearl District atelier. Donut boxes are transported flat in temperature-regulated carriers to prevent glaze sweating or vibration damage during transit.
