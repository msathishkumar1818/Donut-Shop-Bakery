Our Philosophy

The 24-Hour Brioche Difference

Unlike commercial cake donuts mixed in minutes, our dough is treated with the gentle patience of an old-world European boulangerie.

1

Cold Overnight Fermentation

Dough rests for 24 hours at 4°C, developing complex lactic notes and a featherlight airy crumb.

2

Hand Rolling & Precision Proofing

Every ring and pillow is cut by hand with custom brass dies, then slow-proofed in steam chambers.

3

Flash Glazing at 180°F

Dipped warm into freshly simmered natural glazes to lock in supreme moisture without grease.
