Our Origins • Established 2018

Reinventing The Morning Donut as High Patisserie

Founded by Elena Rostova and Julian Thorne, Velvet Dough was born from a singular obsession: to rescue the donut from commercial chemical shortenings and restore it to old-world French brioche excellence.

Our Milestones

A Journey of Patience & Butter

What began with one vintage French copper kettle has grown into a beloved Pacific Northwest culinary staple.

2018

The First Copper Kettle

Elena and Julian launched a 40-donut weekend pop-up at local farmers markets, selling out within 14 minutes.

2021

The 24-Hour Fermentation Mastery

After 300 test batches, we perfected our signature 4°C slow lactic cold ferment, creating a brioche donut that stays featherlight.

2026

Two Flagship Neighborhood Ateliers

Expanding to Pearl District and Hawthorne Garden Courtyard, serving over 1,200 fresh hot rings every morning.

Our Standards

Four Uncompromising Commitments

We will never cut corners on ingredient purity, fermentation time, or craftsmanship ethics.

01

Normandy Cultured Butter

84% butterfat European cultured cream with zero palm oils, hydrogenated fats, or artificial butter flavorings.

02

Stoneground Organic Flour

Single-farm Pacific Northwest wheat milled fresh every 48 hours to preserve living germ oils and roasted grain aroma.

03

24-Hour Cold Fermentation

Patience over haste. Natural sourdough starter enzymes break down starches, creating a digestible, cloud-soft brioche crumb.

04

Botanical Glaze Reductions

Simmered in copper pots using hand-squeezed organic citrus, whole Madagascar vanilla caviar, and field berry purees.
